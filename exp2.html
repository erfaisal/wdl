Patient Check-up Process

When a patient enters the hospital, the following steps are followed:

  1. Patient registration
  2. Meet the receptionist
  3. Visit the doctor
  4. Basic health check-up
    • Check temperature
    • Check blood pressure
    • Check weight
  5. Medical tests, if required
  6. Collect the report
  7. Consult the doctor again
  8. Get medicines and leave
